# Product

## Register

product

## Users

The primary user is the owner and CEO, using an iPhone throughout the day to scan account status, futures positions, watched contracts, Tesla controls, files, and personal tools. The interface should support a quick executive read first and direct access to deeper workflows second.

## Product Purpose

Boss Cockpit is a personal executive command center. It turns account, position, market, vehicle, and file information into a calm daily control surface. Success means the user can understand the current state in seconds without navigating a traditional admin dashboard.

## Brand Personality

Calm, decisive, premium. The product should feel controlled and technically precise, with the confidence of a native iPhone utility and the focus of a high-end vehicle control surface.

## Anti-references

Do not resemble a generic enterprise admin template, a dense KPI wall, a colorful BI dashboard, a neon trading screen, or a grid of interchangeable icon cards. Avoid decorative complexity, excessive labels, and visual effects that compete with executive priorities.

## Design Principles

1. Lead with account, position, and watched-market summaries, not navigation or decoration.
2. Use hierarchy to separate read-only summaries, management pages, and controls.
3. Preserve business meaning and existing data logic while improving scan speed.
4. Make every interaction feel native to one-handed iPhone use.
5. Use restraint to communicate confidence: fewer surfaces, clearer status, stronger spacing.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain readable contrast, 44px minimum touch targets, Dynamic Island and Home Indicator safe areas, reduced-motion support, and status communication that does not rely on color alone. Use the Apple system font stack and keep all critical information legible at iPhone Pro Max widths.
